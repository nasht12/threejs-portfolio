Rigging Bench

Bring in a model from Blender or a download, give it a skeleton, put motion on it, then fill the floor with copies.

1Model

2Rig

No model

Auto-rig fits 19 humanoid bones to the mesh, standing in a T-pose, A-pose or with arms down, and paints the skin weights.

Bone mapping

3Animate

Load a model to see its animations.

4Posture

Adjust how the character holds itself. It applies on top of any animation, and to every copy.

5Instance

Copies share up to 32 distinct poses and draw in one call per material. Walk and jog copies circle their rings.

6Use in a scene

Copy this character, its motion, posture and crowd as a line for the Merced River or Fog Hollow scenes. Paste it into their Guests panel, or near the top of a Director script.

Sable is built into the scenes. Another model has to be added in the scene's Guests panel first, with the same file name.
